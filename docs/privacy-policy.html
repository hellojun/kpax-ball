KPAX Ball Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Summary: KPAX Ball collects minimal data. We only use your Google account email for authentication. We do not sell, share, or monetize your personal data.

1. What We Collect

KPAX Ball collects the following information:

  • Google Account Info: Email address, display name, and profile picture - used solely for authentication via Google Sign-In.
  • Market Data: The Polymarket football markets you analyze (team names, odds) - used to generate AI analysis.
  • Analysis History: Your analysis requests and results - stored on our servers to improve analysis quality over time.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect browsing history beyond Polymarket sports pages.
  • We do not collect financial or trading data.
  • We do not collect passwords or payment information.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking pixels.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Authentication: Your Google email is used to verify your identity and manage access.
  • Analysis: Market data is sent to our backend server to generate AI-powered football analysis.
  • Improvement: Anonymized analysis patterns may be used to improve our AI models.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption).
  • Backend servers are hosted on AWS with standard security practices.
  • Google authentication tokens are managed by Chrome's built-in identity API and are never stored on our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

KPAX Ball uses the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth 2.0: For user authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Polymarket Gamma API: For fetching public market data. No personal data is shared.
  • ZhipuAI (GLM): For AI analysis. Market data (not personal data) is sent for processing.

6. Data Retention

Analysis records are retained for up to 12 months to support accuracy tracking and model improvement. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

  • You can sign out at any time to stop data collection.
  • You can request a copy of your data or its deletion by emailing us.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.

8. Children's Privacy

KPAX Ball is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
